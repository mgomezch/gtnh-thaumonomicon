                About GTNH Thaumonomicon explorer

                Interactive dependency tree explorer, prerequisite checklist, and unlock solver for GT New Horizons Thaumcraft 4 & addon researches, parsed from the GTNH Wiki Research Cheatsheet.

                Navigation & shortcuts

                • Drag / scroll wheel / pinch: Pan and zoom the research tree canvas
                • Click node: Inspect details, required scans, warp, and unlock conditions
                • Double-click node: Set research as the active target tree
                • [ and ]: Collapse or expand left and right sidebars
                • F / C: Fit full graph to screen / center on target node
                • ?: Open or close this about dialog
